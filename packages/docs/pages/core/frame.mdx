## Props

> #### Inherits `BoxProps`

`size?`: SizesString

- The size of the frame
- Default: `96`

`frame?`: 'circle' | 'flower' | 'clover' | 'hexagon' | 'none'

- The shape of the frame
- Default: `'none'`

`theme?`: 'primary' | 'secondary' | 'tertiary' | 'error' | 'none'

- The color theme of the frame
- Default: `'none'`
